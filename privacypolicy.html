  • Account and profile data is retained while your account remains active.
  • Ratings, comments, likes, watchlists, and similar user content are retained until you delete them or delete your account.
  • The notification token is retained for as long as necessary to provide push notification services and may be deleted when you disable notifications or delete your account.
  • Feedback and support correspondence is retained for as long as necessary to resolve your request and comply with potential legal obligations.
  • Security and transaction logs may be retained for as long as necessary for security purposes and in accordance with applicable legal retention periods.

When you delete your account, your account, profile, username, lists, comments, notifications, and following relationships are deleted or anonymized. Copies in service providers’ backups are deleted according to the relevant provider’s backup and deletion cycles. Data that must be retained by law may be kept until the applicable legal retention period ends.

9. Security measures

We apply reasonable technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal data. These measures include:

  • HTTPS/TLS encryption for data transferred between the App and our servers
  • Encryption controls applied during transmission and storage for data stored on Google Firebase infrastructure
  • Secure authentication through Firebase Authentication, Google Sign-In, and Facebook Login
  • Passwords not being visible to us or stored in plain text when registering by email
  • Authorization through authentication tokens and access controls
  • Keeping the server-side TMDB key out of the App
  • Data minimization and collection of only the data necessary for the service

No method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure. Nevertheless, we take appropriate measures to protect personal data against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

If a security breach affecting personal data is detected, the necessary assessment will be carried out in accordance with applicable law, and affected users and competent authorities will be notified where notification is required.
